\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}
\paragraph{VLA adaptation and residual recovery.}
J-PARC is the closest architectural comparison: a frozen VLA, joint-motion
history, inferred fault context, and learned residual action calibration
\citep{jparc2026}, trained with fault labels and paired healthy/faulted
information. Our distinction is online numerical identification from
calibrated proprioceptive residuals, without training a fault-conditioned
residual policy; healthy calibration, sensitivity probes, and fault-family
development remain part of our data budget. TT-VLA uses test-time
reinforcement learning with a pretrained progress estimator
\citep{ttvla2026}, BORA learns a residual through reinforcement learning
\citep{bora2026}, and VLA-Corrector uses visual prediction during inference
\citep{vlacorrector2026}; Residual Control for Fast Recovery from Dynamics
Shifts keeps a nominal policy fixed and learns a bounded, tracking-driven
residual \citep{jayasinghe2026residual}. Our update requires neither
task-progress reward nor tracking feedback, and a matched J-PARC comparison
is needed to establish an empirical advantage.

\paragraph{Adaptive and contraction-based control.}
Disturbance observers and robust adaptation underpin estimation
\citep{chen2016dob,ioannou1996}; composite control combines tracking and
prediction \citep{slotine1989composite}, and informative stored data support
parameter convergence \citep{chowdhary2010concurrent,parikh2019integral}.
Neural-Fly combines learned representations with compact composite adaptation
\citep{oconnell2022neuralfly}, RMA learns legged-dynamics adaptation
\citep{rma2021}, and fault recovery depends on surviving authority
\citep{tao2001actuator}. Contraction gives differential trajectory and
perturbation bounds \citep{lohmiller1998,tsukamoto2021tutorial}; geodesic
adaptive feedback and neural metric constructions are established
\citep{lopez2020adaptivecontraction,tsukamoto2021adaptivecontraction}, and
parameter-dependent metrics need extra transient terms
\citep{gessow2023adaptive}. We specialize this foundation to physical
execution with prediction-only adaptation, biased targets, limited support,
native realization, and delayed application; the composite extension is
unevaluated in the reported VLA trials.
