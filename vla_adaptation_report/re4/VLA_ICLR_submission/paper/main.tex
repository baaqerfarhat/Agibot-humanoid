\section{Discussion and limitations}
The execution metric concerns the robot and its low-level controller under
shared commands, and now has a measured answer: available on a humanoid arm,
absent on the Panda under operational-space control. Manipulation further
requires closed object/contact dynamics or bounds on omitted contact effects;
useful commands, task success, and unmodeled perceptual or contact-topology
change stay outside the guarantee. Measuring the theory's own constants
separated its two halves: the estimator recursions are predictive on the
deployed plant, while its execution certificate is unavailable there.\label{maintext:end}
