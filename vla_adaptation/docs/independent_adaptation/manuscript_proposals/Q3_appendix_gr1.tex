\paragraph{Instantiation on a certified position servo (GR1).}
\label{uniapp:gr1}
On GR1 the right-arm joints $7$--$13$ receive absolute targets at $20$\,Hz and
are corrected on the same joints. Fitting
$q_{k+1}-q_k=a_j\Delta t\,(u_k-q_k)$ per joint on six healthy episodes with
leave-one-episode-out validation gives sampled poles
$\lambda_j=1-a_j\Delta t\in[.72,.77]$ (fold spread $\le.02$) with held-out
increment $R^2$ $.74$--$.95$; the hands and the idle left arm do not certify.
For the same-command comparison in the joint-position metric ($P_x=I$ on
these joints, units rad), $q_{k+1}-q^0_{k+1}=\Lambda(q_k-q^0_k)+(I-\Lambda)(f_k-c_k)$
up to the model remainder, so \eqref{uniapp:physicalpremise} holds with
$\sigma=\max_j\lambda_j=.77$ and $L^v=1-\min_j\lambda_j=.28$ (fold extremes).
An offset fault on the corrected joints is command-equivalent by construction,
so $D_kL_k=I$, $\beta=.28$, $A^f_k=A^c_k=0$ while projection is inactive, and
$\eta^x_k\le.014$\,rad, the certified one-step remainder over the seven joints.
The deployed predictor is a per-joint FIR on position with $K=6$. Its fitted
DC gains are $.968$--$.992$ against probed sensitivities $.990$--$.997$, so
$R=M^{-1}D$ has $\|I-R\|_2=.023$ and the mismatch coefficient attributable to
gain error is $m=.02$; its held-out healthy mismatch $\|M^{-1}r_{k+1}\|_2$
over the seven joints has median $.028$\,rad and 90th percentile $.057$\,rad,
which bounds $\epsilon_k$ at the healthy state. With the innovation law at
gain $\alpha_k=\gamma s_k$, condition \eqref{uni:smallgain-condition} reads
$\beta\alpha\ell<(1-\sigma)\alpha(1-m)$, that is
\begin{equation}
 \ell<\frac{(1-\sigma)(1-m)}{\beta}=.79
 \label{uniapp:gr1condition}
\end{equation}
for any constant gain, and $\ell<.79\,\underline\alpha/\gamma$ for one weight
vector over a gain range $[\underline\alpha,\gamma]$: under a $.10$\,rad
offset the onset gain is $\underline\alpha\approx.012$ ($\ell<.12$) and after
the transient $\underline\alpha\approx.07$ ($\ell<.69$). The remaining
coefficient, $\ell$, the sensitivity of the mismatch to execution deviation
under the healthy-policy comparison, is not measured: the GR1 runs log
estimates but not per-step residuals, and no paired healthy execution exists.
Under the certified model with an exact predictor, $\ell=0$ and the mismatch is
its own decaying transient, $w_{k+1}=\Lambda w_k$; the frozen-fault logs bound
the total coupling $\ell X_k+m E_k$ at $11$--$19\%$ of $\|f\|_2$ (mismatch
medians $.042$ and $.103$\,rad under $.05$ and $.15$\,rad offsets, against
$.028$ healthy) but neither separate $\ell$ from $m$ nor supply $X_k$. With
$\ell=0$ the steady state of \eqref{uni:comparison} is
$E^\ast=\epsilon/(1-m)=.03$--$.06$\,rad and
$X^\ast=(\beta E^\ast+\chi)/(1-\sigma)=.10$--$.13$\,rad; with $\ell=.2$ they
are $.06$--$.10$ and $.13$--$.18$\,rad. The innovation run with a $.10$\,rad
offset reaches $\|\hat f_k-f\|_2=.02$--$.09$\,rad (median $.05$) over steps
$100$--$200$: four of seven episodes lie below the $\ell=0$ value at the
90th-percentile $\epsilon$ and all seven below the $\ell=.2$ value. This
instantiates the constants the corollary needs on one interface; it is not a
certificate for the policy-closed loop, whose replanning every $16$ steps
changes the commands outside the same-command comparison
($\sigma^{16}=.016$, so a within-chunk deviation is forgotten before the next
replan, and the chunk boundary is where policy feedback enters).

Propagating the comparison bound of Theorem~\ref{uni:coupled-theorem} on
twenty logged Spatial episodes with the measured $\sigma=1.00$, the measured
input sensitivity and the healthy residual's 90th percentile as forcing gives
coverage $1.00$ only because the bound never forgets: it grows by $.18$ per
step in the tracking metric and ends at $98$ times (median) the episode's
largest measured residual, and the small-gain margin computed from the same
episodes is negative. These constants are reported as measured; no certificate
follows from them on this interface.
