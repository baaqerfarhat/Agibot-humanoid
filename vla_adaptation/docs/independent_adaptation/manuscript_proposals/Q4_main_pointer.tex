residual and the ARX innovation are related by an invertible filter and carry
the same information about an additive command fault, and under

brackets the settle between $R$ and $1/(2-R)$ according to how much of the
remaining disturbance the policy's own feedback absorbs; the measured
$.32$--$.45$ lies in $[.36,.61]$.
